## Digital wallets & delegated authority (Day 2)

How digital identity wallets work, Ireland's Government Digital Wallet, government credentials (driving licence, PPSN, health identifiers), signing in to public services, and a **draft proposal** for letting one person act for another in the EU Digital Identity Wallet: adults first, then children.
